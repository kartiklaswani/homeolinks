About Homeolinks Homeopathic Clinic

Homeolinks Homeopathic Clinic is a trusted center for classical homeopathic treatment in Vadodara, Gujarat, dedicated to providing safe, gentle, and individualized healthcare since 2002.

Founded by Dr. Kartik Aswani, the clinic is built on the philosophy of treating the root cause of disease rather than merely suppressing symptoms. Over the years, Homeolinks has earned the trust of patients seeking holistic and long-term healing for chronic and recurring health conditions.

At Homeolinks Clinic, every patient is evaluated individually with detailed case analysis, constitutional assessment, and personalized treatment planning. The clinic combines the principles of classical homeopathy with modern clinical understanding to provide compassionate and patient-centered care.

Experienced Homeopathic Doctor in Vadodara

Dr. Kartik Aswani completed his graduation in Homeopathic Medicine from Dr. V. H. Dave Homeopathic Medical College, Anand.

Following graduation, from 2000 to 2002, he gained valuable clinical experience under prominent homeopathic physicians in Vadodara and Mumbai, with extensive exposure at the Dr. M. L. Dhawale Memorial Rural Homoeopathic Hospital — a respected institution known for classical homeopathic education, rural healthcare, and holistic patient care.

Since starting independent practice in 2002, Dr. Kartik Aswani has been actively involved in treating a wide variety of acute and chronic diseases through individualized homeopathic care.
