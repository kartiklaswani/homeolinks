Understanding Hypothyroidism

Hypothyroidism occurs when the thyroid gland produces insufficient thyroid hormones. It commonly leads to symptoms such as fatigue, weight gain, hair fall, dry skin, constipation, swelling, low mood, and menstrual irregularities.

Many patients experience slow metabolism, reduced energy, and difficulty maintaining overall physical and emotional balance.

  • Persistent tiredness
  • Weight gain
  • Hair fall
  • Dry skin
  • Constipation
  • Cold intolerance

Understanding Hyperthyroidism

Hyperthyroidism occurs when the thyroid gland becomes overactive and produces excessive thyroid hormones. Symptoms may include anxiety, palpitations, tremors, weight loss, sweating, irritability, sleep disturbances, and restlessness.
