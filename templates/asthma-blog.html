Understanding Asthma

Asthma occurs when the airways become inflamed and overly sensitive, leading to narrowing of the breathing passages. Symptoms may vary from mild breathing difficulty to repeated asthma attacks affecting daily life and sleep.

Many patients experience worsening of symptoms due to dust, pollution, weather changes, smoke, stress, infections, or allergens.

  • Wheezing and whistling sound
  • Shortness of breath
  • Chest tightness
  • Night-time cough
  • Difficulty breathing during exertion
  • Seasonal worsening
