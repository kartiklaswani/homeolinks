Understanding Eczema

Eczema commonly presents with dry skin, itching, redness, irritation, thickening of skin, scaling, or recurrent eruptions. Symptoms may worsen due to weather changes, stress, allergies, dust exposure, sweating, certain foods, or chemical irritants.

Continuous scratching may lead to skin damage, sleep disturbances, and emotional discomfort.
